About Brahman Origins

Every UBian's education, turned into a world worth exploring.

Most of us only get to live our school years once, moving forward without a way back. Brahman Origins lets players replay that path as a game starting in Elementary, working through High School and Senior High, and arriving, quest by quest, at College. NPCs modeled on the mentors who shape a UB education hand out quests and minigames tied to real subjects, and everything you earn XP, achievements, event rewards stays with one character for the whole journey.

The website you're looking at is the portal around that game: where players sign in, check their progress, and where administrators keep the world running.

The Loyalty Award

The rarest badge in Brahman Origins.

It isn't handed out for a single quest or a lucky minigame run. The Loyalty Award goes only to players who finish the full journey Elementary, High School, Senior High School, and College start to finish, on one account.
