How Brahman Origins works

Nine steps, one running character. Here's exactly what happens between creating an account and holding the Loyalty Award.

01

Create an account

Sign in with your official @ub.edu.ph email. It's the only account type Brahman Origins recognizes — personal Gmail accounts can browse the site but can't play.

02

Enter the game

Click Play the Game from any page to launch the official Unity WebGL build in a new tab. The website and the game share the same account and progress.

03

Explore

Walk the 3D recreations of the UB Elementary and Millennium Campuses, and talk to the NPCs who populate them.

04

Complete quests

Accept quests from NPCs and see them through. Most quest lines unlock the next one once you finish, the way a real course sequence would.

05

Play minigames

Work through educational minigames built around each academic level — short, focused, and worth replaying.

06

Earn XP

Quests, minigames, achievements, and events all add to one XP total. Enough XP moves you up a level; enough levels move you through a stage.

07

Unlock achievements

Hit specific milestones and an achievement unlocks on your profile, timestamped with the date you earned it.

08

Complete your journey

Progress in order: Elementary → High School → Senior High School → College. There's no skipping a stage.

09

Earn the Loyalty Award

Finish College, and the Loyalty Award unlocks on your profile — the one reward reserved for players who saw the whole journey through.

Know the steps. Now walk them.
